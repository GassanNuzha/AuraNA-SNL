Manufacture. Store. Deliver.

Global manufacturing economics with local availability: we hold your production inventory at our North American warehouse and release quantities according to your schedule.

Why local inventory

Buy production quantities. Receive releases.

Overseas manufacturing usually forces a bad choice: order 50,000 pieces and warehouse them yourself, or order small and pay for it in piece price and lead-time risk.

Aura removes the choice. Production runs at economic quantities; finished goods land at our Canadian warehouse; your plant receives releases — weekly, monthly, Kanban-triggered or on demand. Your cash isn't buried in inventory and your line never waits on a ship.

Program options

  • Safety-stock agreements
  • Kanban / replenishment
  • Blanket orders with releases
  • Customer-specific packaging
  • Kitting & consolidation
  • Cross-border logistics
