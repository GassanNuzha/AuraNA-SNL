Plastic Manufacturing

Custom thermoplastic components — injection molded, extruded, overmolded — with mold design, tooling and resin selection managed end to end. You approve samples; we handle everything behind them.

Processes

The right process for the part — not the other way around.

High-volume precision parts from single-cavity prototypes to multi-cavity production tools. Hot runner and cold runner tooling, textures, inserts and living hinges — quoted with tooling amortization options.

How we quote plastics

Tooling is a decision, not a surprise.

Plastic part economics live and die on tooling strategy. A single-cavity tool is cheap but slow; a hot-runner multi-cavity tool is fast but capital-heavy. We quote the alternatives side by side — tool cost, piece price and break-even volume — so you can choose deliberately.

Every molded program includes first-article inspection against the drawing, an approved boundary sample, and process settings locked to that approval.
