Our story

We sat on your side of the desk first.

Aura North America was founded by people who spent years on the buying side — managing purchasing, suppliers, quality claims and inventory for North American manufacturers. We know exactly what it feels like when a supplier goes quiet for two weeks, when a container arrives with the wrong revision, when "it shipped" turns out to mean "it will ship."

So we built the supplier we always wanted: engineering-literate, brutally clear about specifications, obsessive about documentation — and holding stock on this side of the ocean so a delay in transit never becomes a delay on your line.

Aura connects North American manufacturers with qualified international manufacturing partners, and takes full ownership of everything in between: engineering, quoting, tooling, production, inspection, logistics and local inventory.

Straight answers

What we are — and what we're not.

We're deliberately agile.

We don't pretend to be a company with 20,000 employees and fifteen factories. Our advantage is the opposite: small, fast, senior people on every account — with access to a large qualified manufacturing network.

We own the outcome.

Parts are manufactured for Aura North America, inspected against your drawing, and stand behind our name. When you call with a question, the person answering can actually fix things.

We protect your program.

Your drawings and IP are handled under confidentiality, tooling ownership is documented, and country-of-origin and material certifications are disclosed wherever required.

How we operate

The principles behind every program.

/ 01

Specification first

Nothing is produced against a vague email. Drawings, tolerances, finishes and quality requirements are agreed in writing before production.

/ 02

Bad news travels fast

If something slips, you hear it from us first — with a recovery plan, not an excuse.

/ 03

Documentation always

Inspection reports, material certs and traceability on every lot. If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.

/ 04

Engineering honesty

If a tolerance is unnecessary, we'll tell you it's costing you money. If it's not achievable, we'll tell you before tooling.

/ 05

Long programs, not transactions

We're structured for multi-year part programs with stocking agreements — not one-off brokerage.

/ 06

Your customer never waits

Local inventory and scheduled releases exist for one reason: so your production line and your customers never feel the ocean.
