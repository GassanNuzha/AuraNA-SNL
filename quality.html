Your Quality. Our Responsibility.

Every project is managed against agreed specifications, drawings and quality requirements — and documented so you never have to take our word for it.

The Aura quality model

Quality is agreed before production, not argued after.

At quotation, we lock the quality plan with you: which dimensions are critical, what gets inspected at what frequency, which documents ship with each lot. Production then runs against that plan — no ambiguity, no surprises at receiving.

When your program moves to our customer portal, inspection reports and certificates attach to each part number automatically — one click from stock quantity to the paperwork behind it.

Documentation with every program

  • First Article Inspection reports
  • Dimensional reports per lot
  • Material certificates
  • Finish / coating test results
  • Packaging specifications
  • Lot traceability records

Inspection stages

Incoming
Raw material verified against certs before production
First Article
Full dimensional layout on first-off parts (FAI / PPAP on request)
In-Process
Critical dimensions monitored during the run
CMM
Coordinate measurement for tight-tolerance and GD&T features
Final
AQL-based final inspection before packing
Pre-Ship
Packaging and loading inspection with photo record
Receiving (NA)
Verification at our North American warehouse before stocking

AQLstatistically sampled final inspection on every lot
