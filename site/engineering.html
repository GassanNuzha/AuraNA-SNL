Engineering & Product Development

Have a drawing? Great. Only have a sample — or just an idea? We can work from that too. Our engineers turn whatever you have into a fully specified, manufacturable component.

Engineering-led, always

Where every Aura project starts.

Engineering is not a department at Aura — it's the front door. Before anything is quoted or manufactured, our team reviews your part the way a manufacturer would: material, tolerances, process fit, cost drivers and quality risks.

That review pays for itself. A small design-for-manufacturing adjustment before tooling can remove an entire operation from your part — permanently.

Working from any starting point

  • You have full documentation — we validate it for manufacturability and quote directly.
  • You have only a sample — we reverse-engineer it into CAD and drawings you own.
  • You have an idea — we develop the concept into a part that can actually be made, at a cost that makes sense.
