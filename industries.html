Industries We Serve

We focus on industries where we genuinely understand the products, the specifications and the way purchasing works — and go deep instead of wide.

Doors, Windows & Architectural

Aluminum profiles, hinges, handles, locks, rollers, weather seals and die-cast hardware — with architectural finishes and the tolerances glazing systems demand.

  • Extrusions
  • Die-cast hardware
  • Seals
  • Anodize / powder coat

Construction & Building Products

Brackets, anchors, framing components, fabricated assemblies and cast fittings built to spec and stocked for seasonal demand swings.

  • Laser + fabrication
  • Castings
  • Plated steel

HVAC

Fan components, housings, brackets, extruded heat-sink profiles and sheet-metal enclosures — corrosion-protected for mechanical rooms and rooftops.

  • Extrusion
  • Sheet metal
  • Die casting

Automotive & Transportation

Brackets, housings, machined and cast components for vehicle systems, trailers and aftermarket products — with PPAP-level documentation where required.

  • CNC
  • Die casting
  • PPAP support

Industrial Equipment & Machinery

Gear housings, flanges, bases, guards, machine frames and precision machined parts for OEM equipment builders — prototype through production.

  • Machining
  • Fabrication
  • Assemblies

Electrical & Energy

Enclosures, heat sinks, bus components, terminal housings and insulator supports — thermally and electrically specified, finished for the field.

  • Heat-sink extrusions
  • Enclosures
  • Plating

Furniture & Fixtures

Legs, frames, brackets, levelers, plastic glides and decorative metal components — consistent finish quality across production lots.

  • Tube fabrication
  • Injection molding
  • Powder coat

Hardware & Consumer Products

Latches, handles, fasteners, molded housings and finished retail-ready components — packaged to your spec, stocked for your launch curve.

  • Zinc die casting
  • Molding
  • Custom packaging
